## Overview
Filippe Borba presents as an **operator-turned-analyst** pattern: an ownership-driven line leader who spent 13 years running physical fuel-distribution operations and large teams, then deliberately rebuilt his toolkit around data. The behavioral signature is high initiative, decisiveness under operational pressure, and systemic thinking that connects a shop-floor detail to an end-to-end supply chain consequence.

## Mapping to Job Posting Language

When a job posting mentions these keywords, it's a **strong behavioral fit**:
- "ownership", "end-to-end ownership", "autonomy", "self-starter"
- "cross-functional", "business partner", "stakeholder management"
- "supply chain analytics", "control tower", "S&OP", "operations excellence"
- "translate business needs into data", "bridge business and technical teams"
- "continuous improvement", "Lean", "process optimization"
- "remote-first", "async", "distributed team"

When a job posting mentions these, flag as **potential friction** (not deal-breaker):
- "pure research", "no stakeholder contact", "deep ML research" - the value here is applied and business-facing, not research-track
- "5+ years as a Data Scientist / ML Engineer" - a genuine title-level gap; apply with the domain-transfer argument, expect to be screened on it
- "heavily maintenance-only scope", "keep the lights on" - low autonomy and no development path
- "presence required in office daily" - conflicts with the remote-first target
- "PhD required", "publications required" - not the profile

## Management Style Preferences
- Works well with managers who set outcomes and leave the path to him - he has run terminals, he does not need supervision of method
- Values managers who invest in technical development and treat learning time as legitimate work
- Friction expected with micromanagement, presence-based evaluation, and organizations where decisions stall for lack of an owner

## Using This in Applications
- **Cover letters:** Lead with the domain-transfer argument in the first paragraph - 15+ years running the supply chain operations that analytics roles model from the outside. Then the tooling (Python, SQL, Power BI, BigQuery) as the thing that was added, not the thing being learned.
- **CV:** Emphasize scale and ownership (50+ person teams, 60% of a state's fuel supply, 30% of Brazil's liquid bulk volume) alongside the data stack. The combination is the differentiator; either half alone is generic.
- **Interviews:** Use the Vibra progression (MRO Operator to Superintendent) for ownership and growth questions, the Moby control-tower work for analytics and stakeholder questions, and the deliberate re-tooling for "why this transition" questions. See `07-interview-prep.md`.
- **Don't overstate:** Do not claim data science, machine learning, or data engineering roles held by title. Do not claim production ML models, MLOps, or big-data infrastructure engineering. Do not claim fluent Spanish. The honest claim is strong enough: a senior supply chain operator and consultant with real, applied analytics delivery.
