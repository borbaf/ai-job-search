# Behavioral Profile

## Overview
Filippe Borba presents as an **operator-turned-analyst** pattern: an ownership-driven line leader who spent 13 years running physical fuel-distribution operations, liquid bulk terminals, and large teams, then deliberately reinforced his operational authority with a modern analytical layer.

**Core Market Framing: Problem Solver (Resolutor).** The market must see the candidate as a decisive problem solver who resolves operational bottlenecks using data on operations that he personally operated at scale. He is the legitimate bridge between physical execution and executive data intelligence.

## Core Behavioral Drives

| Drive | Level | Meaning |
|-------|-------|---------|
| Ownership / initiative | High | Described as a "protagonist" - moves with end-to-end accountability without waiting for direction |
| Decisiveness | High | Assertive decision-making developed running 50+ person terminal operations where downtime carries direct financial consequence |
| Analytical orientation | High | High analytical drive; connects shop-floor operational reality to cloud data models and executive control towers |
| People orientation | Moderate-High | Strong human side, mentoring, and team management across diverse shop-floor and technical teams |
| Structure / process discipline | Moderate-High | Lean, VSM, 5S, TPM practitioner; rigorous PCM and SAP-based operational discipline |

## Strongest Behaviors
- **Takes ownership end-to-end:** Proven across terminal operations and global equipment delivery (AIS Ireland); acts as the single point of accountability bridging client diagnosis, engineering handoff, and field execution.
- **Decisive problem solver under pressure:** Makes grounded, data-driven decisions when operational tempo is high and assets are critical.
- **Translates between operations and analytics:** Has personally operated the physical supply chain that the data models describe - an undeniable competitive moat over purely academic or theoretical analysts.
- **Continuous technical learner:** Holds electrical engineering and MBA degrees, actively pursues postgraduate studies in Big Data/Data Science, holds Google Cloud certification, and builds public cloud/AI analytics architectures.

## How You Work Best
- Outcome-oriented leadership: managers who set clear strategic targets and leave the operational path and execution method to him.
- Continuous learning environments: cultures that explicitly value technical growth and experimentation.
- Cross-functional bridges: environments requiring tight synergy between operations, commercial strategy, and analytics engineering.
- Remote-first arrangements: structured, asynchronous workflows with clear goals and metrics over physical presence.

## Expected Friction
- Micromanagement and presence-based evaluation.
- Organizations with slow consensus cycles where decisions stall for lack of an explicit owner.
- Maintenance-only scope ("keep the lights on") with no path for architecture development or process optimization.
- Pure theoretical/academic research disconnected from operational stakeholders and business P&L.

## Mapping to Job Posting Language

When a job posting mentions these keywords, it is a **strong behavioral fit**:
- "ownership", "end-to-end ownership", "autonomy", "self-starter"
- "cross-functional", "business partner", "stakeholder management"
- "supply chain analytics", "control tower", "S&OP", "operations excellence"
- "bridge between business and technical teams", "translate operational needs into data"
- "continuous improvement", "Lean", "process optimization"
- "remote-first", "async", "distributed team"
- "problem solver", "data-driven decision making"

When a job posting mentions these, flag as **potential friction** (not deal-breaker):
- "pure research", "no stakeholder contact", "deep ML research" - applied business value is the candidate's core
- "5+ years as a titled Data Scientist / ML Engineer" - title-level gap; bridge with domain authority
- "heavily maintenance-only scope", "keep the lights on" - low autonomy and stagnation risk
- "presence required in office daily" - conflicts with remote-first priority
- "PhD required", "academic publications required" - out of scope

**Strict Anti-Pattern (Veto):**
- **Junior Data Analyst:** Candidate is overqualified with high churn risk. Never target or draft for junior data roles.
